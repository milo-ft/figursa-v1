# Industrias Figursa

Autoclaves, laminar flow hoods, climate chambers, incubators and Kjeldahl apparatus for teaching, research and quality control. Manufacturing, service and spare parts straight from our plant.

## Products

- [Autoclaves](https://figursa.com/en/products/autoclaves/): Steam sterilization for laboratories, clinics and industry.
- [Laminar flow hoods](https://figursa.com/en/products/laminar-flow-hoods/): A sterile work zone with horizontal or vertical flow and HEPA filtration.
- [Climate chambers](https://figursa.com/en/products/climate-chambers/): Controlled temperature and humidity for stability, ageing and growth studies.
- [Fume hoods](https://figursa.com/en/products/fume-hoods/): Operator protection during digestions, distillations and reagent preparation.
- [Kjeldahl apparatus](https://figursa.com/en/products/kjeldahl/): Digestion and distillation for protein and nitrogen determination.
- [Goldfisch fat extractors](https://figursa.com/en/products/goldfisch-fat-extractors/): Fast determination of fats and oils by solvent extraction.
- [Crude fiber apparatus](https://figursa.com/en/products/crude-fiber-apparatus/): Crude fiber in feed, food and agricultural products by reflux.
- [Soxhlet extractors](https://figursa.com/en/products/soxhlet-extractors/): Six heating units for extractions, distillations and digestions.
- [Incubators](https://figursa.com/en/products/incubators/): Culture incubators: forced-air, BOD, low-temperature and orbital.

- [Dry-block tube incubators](https://figursa.com/en/products/dry-block-incubators/): Dry blocks from ambient to 130 °C, 1 to 6 blocks.

- [Water baths](https://figursa.com/en/products/water-baths/): Constant temperature for tubes, circulation, shaking and tissue flotation.
- [Heating mantles](https://figursa.com/en/products/heating-mantles/): For round-bottom flasks from 50 mL to 22 L and resin reactors.
- [Hot plates and stirrers](https://figursa.com/en/products/hot-plates-stirrers/): Hot plates, magnetic stirrers and combined units.
